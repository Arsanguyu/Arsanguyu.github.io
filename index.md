## My Favorite CPSC 120L Labs

* Lab 8 - Part 1

    I liked this lab since it introduced the idea and concepts of vectors. It also helped in strengthening my ability to use for loops as well as if statements which gave me insight into how I could exclude certain values within a vector, especially minimum and maximum values. Overall, I enjoyed seeing how command line arguments can be used in order to give an output with the various functions that we created.

* Lab 9 - Part 1

    Lab 9 was one of my favorite labs as it utilized text contained within multiple source files. This lab showed me that our programs do not need to be one dimensional in the sense that the values being used are contained within one source file using functions from other files. Which was not only really fun to do but also very satisfying when the program outputted the various spelling mistakes from each text file.

* Lab 10 - Part 1

    Lastly, Lab 10 was a favorite lab of mine since it built upon our knowledge of nested loops. Along with this came the introduction on the idea of two dimensional vectors, which further expanded on the capabilities of vectors. Demonstrating their usefulness in storing information which may contain more than one value of information.
